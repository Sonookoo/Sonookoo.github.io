Text-based Speech Editing Demo

Description

This demo showcases our research on text-based speech editing systems. We utilize the VCTK Dataset as our primary source of speech data. Our approach builds upon and compares with previous works, including A3T [1] and EditSpeech [2], which have laid the foundation for controllable and realistic speech editing via text input.
